Delete your Filmarr account

Applies to the Filmarr app by Green Forrest (package app.greenforrest.filmarr)

Delete from inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Filmarr and go to Settings → Delete account.
  2. Tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete forever.

Deletion runs immediately on our servers and cannot be undone.

Or request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email mehdiacho@gmail.com from the address associated with your Filmarr account, with the subject "Filmarr account deletion request". We verify the request comes from your account's email, then permanently delete your account and all associated data. Requests are honoured within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in credential (email/password or the Google sign-in link) and Firebase account.
  • Everything tied to your taste: hearted films, your library, watched / watching / plan-to-watch marks, ratings, swipe history, and genre interests.
  • Your profile data, including the optional age bracket.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing is retained by Filmarr after deletion completes.
  • Filmarr has no in-app purchases and never stores any payment details.
  • Film details and posters shown in the app come from TMDB and are public catalogue data — they are not your personal data and are unaffected by deletion.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

Within the app you can remove individual items yourself at any time — un-heart a film, remove a title from your library, or change a watched/rating mark — without deleting your whole account.
