Filmarr — Privacy Policy

Effective: 15 July 2026 · Applies to the Filmarr Android app (package app.greenforrest.filmarr)

Filmarr is a film-discovery app: you swipe through films, build a watchlist and a taste graph, and get recommendations tuned to what you love — no searching required. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the control you have over it. The short version: your data is yours — we process it only to provide the features you use, we don't sell data, and we don't show ads.

Data we collect and why

  • Account information. If you sign in: your email address, and (with Google Sign-In) your name and profile photo from your Google account; or the email address you register with. You can browse films as a guest without an account — personal features (your feeds, library, and profile) require signing in.
  • Your taste and activity. The signals you give the app: films you heart, add to your library, or mark as watched / watching / plan-to-watch, your ratings, the swipe choices you make, and the genres you pick during onboarding. This is what your recommendations are built from.
  • Optional age bracket. If you choose to provide it, a coarse age range (e.g. 25–34) — used only to improve recommendations. It is optional and you can skip it.

How processing works

  • Storage and sync. Your account and taste data live in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in a per-user area only your signed-in account can access, in the europe-west1 region. Data is encrypted in transit.
  • Recommendations. To generate your feeds, your interaction data is processed by our recommender backend (Google Cloud Run), which acts as a service provider on our behalf. It is used only to compute your recommendations, not to identify you, and is not shared with anyone for their own purposes.
  • Film metadata. Film details, posters, and artwork are fetched from TMDB (The Movie Database), a third-party content provider. The app requests public catalogue data from TMDB; your account or taste data is not sent to TMDB.

What we don't do

  • No selling of personal data. No advertising, and no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases — Filmarr is free, and we never handle payment details.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or messages.

Your controls

  • Delete your data / account. You can permanently remove your account and all your Filmarr data — see the deletion page.

Retention

Your account and taste data are retained until you delete your account. Once deletion completes, nothing is retained by Filmarr.

Children

Filmarr is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes
